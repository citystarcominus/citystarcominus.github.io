About Us

City Star Machinery & Construction Sdn. Bhd. is a Malaysia-based construction machinery rental company, built on trust and reliability.

Our head office is in Taman Mount Austin, one of the busiest areas in Johor Bahru. Since 2014, we've been supplying construction machinery across the JB and Klang Valley (KL) areas, earning a reputation as a dependable partner for contractors who need equipment they can count on.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver and service machinery across Johor Bahru and the Klang Valley (Kuala Lumpur) area.

Do you repair machinery you did not rent out?

No, our in-house repair team maintains and repairs our own rental fleet. We do not offer repair services for machinery rented or purchased elsewhere.

How do I get a rental quote?

Send us the equipment type, rental duration and site location on WhatsApp and we'll reply with a quote.

What types of machinery do you rent?

We rent excavators, backhoe loaders, dump trucks, road rollers, cranes, telehandlers, hydraulic breakers and other construction machinery.

How long has City Star Machinery been operating?

City Star Machinery & Construction Sdn. Bhd. has been supplying construction machinery since 2014, based in Taman Mount Austin, Johor Bahru.
